My project focuses on visualizing how electric vehicles play a role in reducing greenhouse gas emissions and fossil fuel consumption over the period 2020–2025. The data for this project is gathered from the Centraal Burea voor de Statistiek (CBS) Statline.For the visualization, I will employ different data plotting libraries such as Matplotlib, Plotly Express and Seaborn, as these will offer me different statistical ways of plotting my data and making my graphs interactive. For the graphical plots, the total number of electric vehicles will be plotted against various variables, such as, the total amount of fossil-based vehicles, or the total amount of greenhouse gases emitted. By obtaining and comparing these different graphs, I expect to see a general trend of positive influence from electric vehicles in reducing greenhouse gas emissions. Additionally, if possible I would like to explore whether bio-based fuels had an impact on reducing greenhouse gas emissions, as this data is also available within the dataset I will use.
